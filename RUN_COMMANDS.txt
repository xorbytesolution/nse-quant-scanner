2) RUN NSE STOCK QUANT SCANNER (CLI TERMINAL MODE)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
python nse_scanner.py

What it does:
- Automatically downloads live active stock symbols from NSE archives (2,300+ symbols).
- Uses multi-threading (25 workers) for fast parallel analysis.
- Evaluates 17 strict quantitative technical indicators & breakout conditions:
  1-7. 7-Day Daily Range Expansion (Today High-Low > Past 7 Days)
  8. Bullish Daily Candle (Close > Open)
  9. Day-over-day Gain (Close > Prev Close)
  10. Bullish Weekly Candle (Weekly Close > Weekly Open)
  11. Bullish Monthly Candle (Monthly Close > Monthly Open)
  12. Volume Filter (Yesterday Volume > 10,000)
  13. SMA Alignment (SMA20 > SMA40)
  14. SMA Alignment (SMA40 > SMA60)
  15. Gap Down Discount Open (Open < Yesterday Close)
  16. Ichimoku Senkou Span B Cloud Breakout (Close > Span B 9,26,52)
  17. Parabolic SAR Bullish Trend (PSAR < Close)
- Saves output table to: scanner_results.csv (sorted by Volume descending).
